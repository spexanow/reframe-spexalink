Before you apply

What happens on the Discovery/Match Call?

It's sixty minutes. Bring one real challenge and Mona will reframe it with you, so you leave with something useful either way. Then you both decide whether working together makes sense. If she isn't the right person for what you need, she'll tell you, and recommend someone who is.

"I don't have time for this."

Fair. Your calendar is part of what you'd be fixing. This doesn't add a project. You work on what's already on your desk: the decision you keep putting off, the conversation you're rehearsing, the board meeting next week. When something comes up between conversations, a short voice note is often enough to reframe it before it costs you a day.

"I already have people to think with."

Keep them. Each of them has a stake in you: your board evaluates you, your team depends on you, your investors have their own timeline. A good peer group gives you breadth. It can't give you depth on your team, inside your company. Mona has no vote on your future. Her only interest is your success.

Is what we discuss confidential?

Yes. What you talk about stays between you and Mona, even when your company is paying.
