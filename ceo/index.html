How Mona works

You work on a live challenge, not a case study. Each round is short and practical.

  1. Bring a real challenge

    The one on your desk this week.

  2. See how you're seeing it

    Mona listens to the story you're telling, then breaks its logic with "what if" questions.

  3. Pick the option that stretches you

    New options appear. You choose one that's a real stretch and try it with your team.

  4. Test it, then debrief

    You try it that week and look at what happened, like a researcher looks at data. Then again.

The first shift often comes in a single conversation. Repeating it is what makes it stick.

Questions leaders ask

I don't have time for this.

Your calendar is part of what you'd be fixing. This doesn't add a project. You work on what's already on your desk: the decision you keep putting off, the conversation you're rehearsing, the board meeting next week. Between conversations, a short voice note is often enough to reframe something before it costs you a day.

I already have a board, advisors and a peer group.

Keep them. Each of them has a stake in you: your board evaluates you, your team depends on you, your investors have their own timeline. A peer group gives you breadth. It can't give you depth on your team, inside your company.

Isn't coaching for leaders who aren't performing?

The leaders Mona works with are performing. They keep someone in their corner for the reason elite athletes do: the stakes are high and they want an edge.

How does working with Mona work, and what does it cost?

It's a premium, one-to-one engagement, priced like a senior advisor. You work on live decisions in regular conversations, with short voice notes in between when something comes up. If the Reframe Check call shows it's a fit, Mona walks you through how it would work for you and the investment.

If my company or investors pay, what do they hear?

The goals you agree together, never the content. What you say stays between you and Mona.
