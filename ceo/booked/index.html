What happens in the hour

Sixty minutes, on video. You leave with something useful, whatever you decide.

First 10 min
You describe the challengeIn your own words. Mona mostly listens, because how you describe a problem is where the frame shows up.
Next 30 min
Mona reframes it with you, liveA run of "what if" and "I wonder" questions. Some feel obvious, some uncomfortable. New options tend to appear when the old logic stops holding.
Next 10 min
You pick one experimentOne thing to try with your team this week. Small and real.
Last 10 min
You both decide on next stepsWhether working together makes sense. If Mona isn't the right person for what you need, she'll say so and recommend someone who is.

Make the hour count

A quiet roomSomewhere you can talk freely for the full hour, with your camera on.
No slidesCome as you are. The challenge and your honest view of it are enough.
Just youThis first conversation works best one-to-one. Others can join later if you work together.

What you share stays between you and Mona.

That holds even if your company or investors pay for any work that follows. They would hear the goals you agree together, never the content.
